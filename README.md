# Credit Card Fraud Detection using Machine Learning

Most payment fraud by value arose from credit transfers and card payments. More specifically, in 2024 the total value of fraudulent credit transfers sent by payment service providers (PSP2) in EU/EEA amounted to EUR 2.5 billion, with a fraud rate of 0.001%, and the value of fraudulent card transactions in the EU/EEA amounted to EUR 1.3 billion, with a rate of 0.033% [1]. In other words, fraud associated with credit, debit, and prepaid cards is a significant and growing issue for consumers, businesses, and the financial industry.
